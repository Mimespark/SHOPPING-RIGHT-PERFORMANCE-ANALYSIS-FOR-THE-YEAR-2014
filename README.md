INTRODUCTION

The data used in this analysis comes from a lesson at Vephla University, detailing goods sold by each salesperson across various cities where the grocery store are located for the year 2014. The data was thoroughly cleaned, processed and analyzed using Microsoft Excel. The dataset consists of multiple columns which I grouped under two (2) categories:

INDEPENDANT DATA VARIABLES; Customer name, Salesperson, Region, Ship name, Ship country, Payment type, Product name, Category and City.

DEPENDANT DATA VARIABLES; Quality, Revenue, Unit Price and Shipping Price.

Before starting this analysis, I mapped out a plan to pinpoint key insights and possible analysis, which guided me in building an interactive analytical dashboard.

ANALYSIS OBJECTIVE

To evaluate the revenue performance of customers, salespersons, cities, product categories, and regions for the year 2014, in order to identify sales trends, top customers, region, salespersons and areas of growth for the business.

KEY OBSERVATIONS AND FINDINGS

For the Salesperson Performance: The highest revenue was generated by Nancy Freehafer with a total of $104,242.34. Anne Larsen and Andrew Cencini followed with $93,848.33 and $67,180.50, respectively.

For the Customer Performance: The top three customers by revenue contribution: Company D: $67,180.50, Company H: $50,198.35 and Company BB: $43,703.00.

For the Regional Performance: The highest sales revenue came from the North region ($141,660.34), followed by the East ($108,275.51) and South ($93,848.33).

For the Monthly Sales Trends Performance: January recorded the highest revenue at $32,907.84, followed by March at $30,852.60. February had the lowest revenue of $19,955.50, indicating a potential seasonal dip.

The Sales by Product Category analysis: Beverages generated the highest revenue ($110,577.11), followed by Sauces ($69,000) and Jams & Preserves ($51,541).

Transaction Distribution Majority of transactions fell within the $0-$1,000 range (218 transactions). Higher value transactions ($2,000-$3,000) were significantly fewer (31 transactions).

Top Cities by Revenue and Shipping: New York led in revenue generation with $67,180.50, followed by Portland ($50,198.35) and Miami ($50,145.33). Shipping data aligns with revenue trends, suggesting key market locations.
